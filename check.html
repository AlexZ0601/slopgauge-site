How it works

Every sentence is scored with its neighbours, about 60 words at a time.

A small model trained on about 120,000 texts by people and by GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Llama and others reads word choice, word pairs, punctuation and sentence rhythm, together with Slopgauge's named slop patterns. The verdict scores the whole text in parts of about 220 words.

Tested on 1,621 essays, stories and news articles it never saw, including essays by English learners: it called 1% of the human-written texts "Likely AI-written" and caught 87% of the AI-written ones. In texts that were half human and half AI, it marked 64% of the AI half and 4% of the human half.

Short texts are harder: under a few hundred words, results are less reliable. How the model was built.
