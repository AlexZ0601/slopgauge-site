    Nothing you read leaves your browser.

    Slopgauge measures text on your device and sends it nowhere. No servers, no account, no analytics. The gauge on this page runs in your browser too.

    The one exception is Google Docs, which draws its text on a canvas: Slopgauge downloads the open doc as plain text from Google, the same as File > Download, and reads it locally.
