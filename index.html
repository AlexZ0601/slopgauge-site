See the slop before you read it.

Slopgauge is a free Chrome extension that puts a slop meter on the posts you read, from your LinkedIn feed to your inbox. Hover the meter to see which phrases gave it away.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

Free. Runs on your device. No account.

How it works

Chatbot writing has tells, from its favorite words to its favorite setups. Slopgauge counts them.

A meter on every post

Every post gets a meter, from “No slop” to “Pure slop”, as you scroll. There is nothing to click.

The evidence, on hover

Hover a meter to see each pattern it found and why it reads as generated. Flagged phrases are highlighted in the post.

A quieter feed

Turn on “Dim heavy slop” to fade the worst posts. Hover one to read it anyway.

What the levels mean

The meter counts named patterns. Long texts are scaled, so an essay isn't flagged just for being long.

✓ No slop

Nothing that reads like chatbot writing.

A little slop

One pattern. People write like this too.

Some slop

Two patterns.

Heavy slop

Three or four. Rare in writing people do by hand.

Pure slop

Five or more.

We tested it on 668 news articles written from the same headlines: 268 by journalists and 400 by AI models. 21% of the AI-written articles scored Heavy slop or worse. None of the human-written ones did. It counts patterns; it can't prove who wrote something.

Anywhere else, select text and right-click “Check with Slopgauge”, or paste it into the toolbar popup.

Private by design

Slopgauge runs entirely in your browser. It has no servers and never sends anything you read to anyone. Read the privacy policy.
