Questions about Pro

Will the free version stay free?

Yes. Everything that is free today stays free: the extension, the essay checker, comparing with known writing, and checking a class.

Do you see my students’ essays?

No. Pro keeps your classes in this browser, on this computer. The only thing sent anywhere is your license key, to Polar, to check that it’s valid. Clearing this site’s data, or deleting a class, removes its essays for good.

How does the comparison with earlier essays work?

When you save an assignment to a class, each student’s essay is kept with their name. Next time, each new essay is compared with that student’s earlier essays that read as their own, by a neural style model (LUAR) running in your browser. It answers “Sounds like them”, “Hard to tell” or “Doesn’t sound like them”. In testing, a writer’s own text was called “Doesn’t sound like them” 2 times in 100. A different-sounding essay can have honest reasons, so treat it as a reason to talk.

Who handles the payment?

Polar, the merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax, and emails your license key. Slopgauge gets your name and email to manage your license, never your card.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes, enter your key on each one. Saved classes stay on the computer where you saved them, because they’re never uploaded.

Can I cancel, or get a refund?

Cancel any time from the link in your receipt, or with “Manage your subscription” above. If Pro isn’t for you, email support@slopgauge.com within 14 days for a full refund.

Our school needs an invoice, a W-9 or a privacy agreement.

Email support@slopgauge.com with your school’s name and what your district needs. Because student work never leaves teachers’ computers, there is no student data for Slopgauge to store, share or lose.
